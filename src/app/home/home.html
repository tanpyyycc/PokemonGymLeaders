Welcome to the Pokémon Region Archives

Explore the historical regions of the Pokemon World, where legendary Gym Leaders test the bonds between trainers and their Pokémon.

Kanto Region

Known as the traditional starting ground for aspiring Pokémon League Champions, modeled after the real Kantō Region in Japan, Kanto's starting route, Pallet Town is modeled of the rural areas, features eight classic Gyms spanning from Pewter City to Viridian City, steeped in rich early-league history.

Johto Region

Connected directly to Kanto, it's western neighbour that has a contiguous landmass on the south via historical pathways, Johto is a region rich in tradition, ancient towers, and unique cultural bonds between people and their partner Pokémon.
