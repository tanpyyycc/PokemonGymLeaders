Welcome to the Pokémon Region Archives

Explore the historical regions of the Pokemon World, where legendary Gym Leaders test the bonds between trainers and their Pokémon.

Kanto Region

Known as the traditional starting ground for aspiring Pokémon League Champions, modeled after the real Kantō Region in Japan, Kanto's starting route, Pallet Town is modeled of the rural areas, features eight classic Gyms spanning from Pewter City to Viridian City, steeped in rich early-league history.

Johto Region

Connected directly to Kanto, it's western neighbour that has a contiguous landmass on the south via historical pathways, Johto is a region rich in tradition, ancient towers, and unique cultural bonds between people and their partner Pokémon.

Hoenn Region

Inspired by the island of Kyushu and home of the ancestral pokemon "Groudon", it is located southwest of Kanto and Johto. In terms of it's history, 1,000 years prior, the former king of Kalos, AZ travelled to Hoenn and planted a tree in front of the Cave of Origin's entrance. A primal energy-filled meteor struck the region, creating a gigantic crater where Sootopolis City will be built in. Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre awakened to fight over it once again. The people of Hoenn wished on Rayquaza to save them once again, and using a special meteorite, Rayquaza performed the world's first Mega Evolution, defeated the two, took away their Primal states, and forced them back to sleep again. The battle was also witnessed by AZ, learning even more about Mega Evolution. Later on, the Sky Pillar was built in Rayquaza's honor and the Draconids promised to pass down the prophecy that in 1,000 years the meteor would return again.
